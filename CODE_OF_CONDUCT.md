# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- demonstrating empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- focusing on what is best for the community and project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insults, publishing another person's private information, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Responsibilities and scope

Maintainers may clarify and enforce these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, issues, and other participation that violates them. These standards apply in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report project conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers using the private contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile. For urgent platform abuse or threats, use GitHub's content-reporting and safety channels. Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive information.

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the privacy and safety of everyone involved. Maintainers who are the subject of a report must recuse themselves from handling it. Corrective action may range from a private warning or removal of content to a temporary or permanent ban, depending on impact and context.
